# Brightlearn-Coffee-Final
Coffee shop activities analysis

**Introduction**
Bright Coffee Shop has established itself as a vibrant community hub dedicated to serving high-quality coffee and artisanal pastries. As the local coffee market grows, understanding the intricate details of daily transactions is essential to maintaining competitive advantage and operational efficiency. This report provides a comprehensive analysis of sales data from  January 2023 to June 2023, exploring key performance metrics such as total revenue, transaction volumes, and product popularity. By leveraging this data, Bright Coffee Shop aims to shift from intuitive decision-making to data-driven strategies, optimizing both profitability and customer satisfaction.

**Objectives**
The primary purpose of this analysis is to transform raw sales records into actionable insights that optimize operational efficiency and maximize revenue for Bright Coffee Shop.

Key Objectives include:
Sales Performance Trends: Analyze total revenue trends across days, weeks, and months to identify peak hours and seasonality patterns.
Product Performance Evaluation: Identify top-selling items and underperforming products to refine the menu and optimize inventory management.
Customer Behavior Insights: Determine peak traffic times to optimize staff scheduling and reduce wait times, enhancing the overall customer experience.
Operational Optimization: Identify high-growth areas and opportunities to maximize profitability, such as tailoring promotions based on popular product combinations.
Data-Driven Decision Making: Provide recommendations that empower management to make informed choices on inventory, staffing, and marketing strategies.
Best-selling Items: Which coffees or pastries bring in the most revenue?
Peak Timing: What are the busiest times 
Weekdays vs. Weekends: When are the highest transaction volumes?
Product Mix: Are customers pairing coffee with food?
